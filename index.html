01 — About

Legacy pipelines in. Governed lakehouses out.

I'm a Senior Data Engineer at Tata Consultancy Services in Bengaluru, where I've spent 4+ years on the unglamorous but decisive half of analytics: making the data arrive on time, in the right shape, and provably correct.

My work sits at the intersection of Snowflake warehousing, distributed processing with Spark/PySpark, and Python-driven ETL/ELT modernisation. I own delivery end to end — requirements, Agile execution through Azure DevOps Boards/Repos/Pipelines, release management, and production support. Semiconductor Yield Intelligence Pipeline
